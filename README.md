# LifeStack Privacy Policy

For the LifeStack app on Meta's platform. Effective 27 October 2026.

LifeStack is a private tool that LifeStack Apps uses to manage its own Facebook Page, [LifeStack Apps](https://www.facebook.com/lifestackapps). It is not offered to the public: nobody else can log in to it, and it does not act on anyone else's account or Page.

This policy explains what information the app handles when it works with our Page and what we do with it. It covers only this Meta app. Our Android app, Stash: Envelope Budget, has its own privacy policy.

## What the app does

- Publishes, schedules and edits posts on the LifeStack Apps Page.
- Reads the Page's own posts and their aggregate performance numbers (Page and post insights such as views, clicks, reactions and follows).
- Reads comments left on the Page's posts, and can reply to or hide them, so we can respond to people.

## Information we handle

- **Our own Page content and settings**: posts, images, captions and schedules we create.
- **Aggregate insights**: totals and counts that Meta reports for the Page and its posts. These do not identify individual people.
- **Comments on our Page**: when we review comments, the app receives the comment text and the commenter's public name and ID as Facebook provides them. This information is already visible on our public Page.
- **Access tokens** that let the app act for our Page. They are stored only on the administrator's own computer and are never published or shared.

The app does not collect information from people who visit our Page but do not comment, and it does not access private messages, friend lists, contact details or any personal profile.

## How we use it

- To publish and manage our Page's posts.
- To measure how our posts perform so we can improve our content. We keep these results as aggregate figures.
- To read and respond to comments on our posts.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, build profiles of individuals, or combine it with other data about people.

## Who processes it

- **Meta** hosts our Page and provides the information through its Graph API, under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).
- **Anthropic**: we use Claude, an AI assistant, to help draft posts, review performance and read comments. Information the app retrieves, which can include comment text and commenters' public names, may be processed by Anthropic to provide that service, under [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

No one else receives information from the app.

## How long we keep it

Comments are read when they are needed and are not stored by us beyond that review. We keep records of our own posts (their IDs, dates and aggregate performance figures) for as long as they are useful for planning our marketing. Access tokens are kept until they are replaced or revoked.

## Your choices and data deletion

You can edit or delete your own comments on our Page at any time on Facebook. If you would like us to delete any information about you that we hold through this app, email us at [jebb.domingo@gmail.com](mailto:jebb.domingo@gmail.com) with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access to any information through your Facebook settings.

## Security

Access tokens are kept only on the administrator's computer, outside any shared or public location, and the app's ability to delete content is turned off. No method is completely secure, but we limit access to the people who manage the Page.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
